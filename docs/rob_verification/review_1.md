## Consequential findings

JBI item 6 concerns identification of the employment/education condition and item 7 its standard reliable measurement. Numerous original rationales instead appraised SCID/ICD diagnostic eligibility. Those rationales are replaced with employment-specific evidence. Bonnesen identification changes from unclear to yes because DREAM has an explicit administrative work/education rule; Jirapramukpitak changes from no to yes because paid annual personal earnings are operationally specified. This does not resolve whether all recruited participants meet diagnostic eligibility. Jirapramukpitak's frame remains uncertain for that reason. Bonnesen's exclusion of baseline disability-pension recipients means its frame is inappropriate for general psychosis employment prevalence, although valid for its expressly pension-free cohort.

The Mayoral employment-category counts sum to 156 at both baseline and one year despite 157 entered participants. Baseline percentages divide by 156; follow-up percentages divide by 157. The observed employment-status denominator is 156. A documented suicide cannot be assigned as the missing employment observation because baseline already sums to 156 and no explicit linkage is supplied. This corrects the original response-rate rationale; the omitted status remains unexplained.

RoB 2 deviations were re-examined for effects of assignment. Open treatment, low fidelity, evolution of usual care and unavailable outcome data do not automatically establish trial-context deviations. Greenwood and Killackey domain 2 become low. Killackey's workshops occurred before the current trial and its high overall judgement remains supported by directly outcome-dependent withdrawal under domain 3. Rodriguez-Pulido domain 2 becomes low because its completer exclusion is missing follow-up, while two completers below the adherence threshold remain included. Overall concerns remain, without source evidence demonstrating the original high deviations rating. Zhang's unknown dropout handling supports provisional some concerns rather than an automatic high judgement. Its explicit masked, common outcome procedure supports low measurement risk for the thresholded construct; construct/window mismatch is a separate issue. Incorrect and unrelated BPRS claims are removed from the employment selective-reporting rationale. Prospective registries/protocols not held or inspected are not treated as verified merely because the source cites them.

Stouten reports continuous PSP severity of problems in study/work/socially useful activity. It does not report employment prevalence. Prevalence-specific sample-size, condition identification, condition measurement and prevalence-analysis items are marked not applicable rather than asserting poor validity of its continuous scale or appropriate continuous-outcome analysis. Other recorded JBI items remain descriptions of its recruitment and reporting; their presence does not constitute a valid complete prevalence appraisal.

Strålin's 67/161 employment-income-threshold category is explicit. Its 86/161 any-income text does not sufficiently state that every income component is employment income, so its employment identification and measurement are unclear. Strassnig 2017 retains severe irreconcilable employment counts/percentages and an abstract/results sample-size conflict. Strassnig 2018 retains shifted percentages and non-reproducing printed chi-square results. Diagnostic subgroup restriction alone is not proof of non-coverage; eligible-to-observed coverage is uncertain without the referenced flow. Turner's education participation includes participants also employed, so it cannot establish an exclusive education-only result;38/39 entry and participation-component discrepancies remain.

JBI sample-size adequacy lacks a common documented precision criterion across this corpus. Modest original yes decisions based only on sample size are changed to unclear where no stated precision justification establishes adequacy. Existing small-sample concerns and clearly large-register descriptions must still be read as qualitative, provisional judgements rather than approval of an unstated numerical threshold. A common criterion needs adjudication across all allocations before sample-size ratings are used inferentially.

## Applicability and remaining limits

The existing CSVs contain domain ratings without complete signalling responses, target-trial specifications or algorithm traces. This audit checks their factual basis and proposes source-grounded judgements; it does not retroactively supply a complete RoB 2 or ROBINS-I administration. Cluster RoB 2 requirements apply to Greenwood. ROBINS-I concerns relate to non-randomised intervention contrasts and cannot be assumed to validate isolated arm prevalences. Morpheus exposure starts after OPUS baseline, and cumulative 0-2 year outcomes include some pre-intervention time. Its any-employment 48-month label actually describes the separate 2-4 year period, not cumulative 0-4 years. Those estimand/clock issues remain distinct from objective uniform register recording.

Coverage is defined by the 496 pre-existing assigned assessment rows. It does not cover outcomes with no assessment rows, including the unassessed Evensen subsidiary vocational categories, Martini conditional retention outcome and Jirapramukpitak whole-cohort result. Externally cited protocols, registrations, supporting flowcharts and validation studies were not all retrieved. The supplement inspected locally was `dissertation_shared_folder/supplements/Jirapramukpitak 2022 12888_2022_3888_MOESM1_ESM.docx`. No frozen extraction shard or merged scientific table was edited. Parent integration must preserve the originals and adjudicate the proposed changes through the verification overlay.
